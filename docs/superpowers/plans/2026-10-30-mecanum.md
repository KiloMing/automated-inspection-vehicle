**Tech Stack:** Jazzy, Harmonic, gz_ros2_control, Nav2 MPPI/AMCL Omni, Python.
**Spec:** ../specs/2026-10-30-mecanum-design.md

## Global Constraints
- Wildfire LubanCat 5 RK3588S, Ubuntu 24.04, ROS 2 Jazzy; STM32 owns four motors.
- Preserve legacy packages, firmware, lidar sources and mapping evidence.
- No invented hardware implementation or hardware verification claims.

## Review Focus
- Wheel order/sign and geometry mismatch: basis vectors and round trip tests.
- Lateral motion accidentally disabled downstream: Nav2/controller configuration tests.
- Competing TF publishers or mismatched Twist type: explicit ownership and launch inspection.
- Stale/nonfinite command inputs: bounded smoke commands, finite validation, timeout tests.
- Missing installed assets/dependencies: package build, Xacro expansion, launch argument checks.
